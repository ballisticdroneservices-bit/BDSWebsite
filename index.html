About BDS

I’m Jason Panchuk, owner and pilot at Ballistic Drone Services, based in Ladysmith BC. Drawing on my background in the Canadian Armed Forces, as well as years of construction and safety experience, I bring discipline, precision, and attention to detail to every flight. BDS delivers crisp aerial photography, cinematic video, and dependable inspection data for clients across Vancouver Island and BC.

  • Efficient, safe operations—including work near people and structures where permitted.
  • Clear deliverables: RAW/JPEG, 4K video, orthos, and annotated PDFs.
  • Friendly, reliable communication from brief to delivery.
